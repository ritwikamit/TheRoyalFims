## Brand & Style

This design system embodies high-end cinematic wedding filmmaking, luxury visual storytelling, and bespoke couture celebrations. Designed for couples, visionary wedding planners, and cinematic connoisseurs, the aesthetic balances the timeless gravity of a screening room with the refined editorial presence of fine-art publications.

The aesthetic philosophy draws heavily on **Minimalism** enriched by **Dark Cinematic Atmosphere** and delicate glass overlays. Deep, nuanced obsidian surfaces (`#000000`, `#111111`) evoke the hushed intimacy of theatrical auditoriums, allowing dynamic 4K moving pictures and still imagery to become the primary radiant light sources. High-contrast typography, restrained crimson/ruby flourishes (`#D60000`), and muted amber-gold gilding (`#FAD189`) establish an atmosphere of regal grandeur, emotional depth, and uncompromising artistic precision.

## Colors

The palette operates in a default dark cinematic mode, treating darkness not as an absence of light, but as an expansive theatrical canvas:

- **Primary (`#d60000` / Crimson Royale):** The evocative heartbeat of the palette. Reserved for primary film triggers, VIP calls to action, active chapter trackers, and emotional focal points.
- **Secondary (`#fad189` / Champagne Gold):** A soft, luminous metallic tone used for editorial metadata, film awards laurels, director credits, and delicate micro-borders.
- **Tertiary (`#f59e0b` / Warm Amber):** An intense, golden-hour amber reserved for highlighting premiere dates, exclusive booking ribbons, and active state accents.
- **Neutral (`#111111` / Film Noir Canvas):** The foundational tone of structural containers, flanked by pure `#000000` for canvas ground and `#1a1a1a` for elevated floating cards.

Text utilizes stark pure whites and warm neutral tones to maintain optimal contrast across low-light screening interfaces.

## Typography

Typography establishes an interplay between high-fashion couture editorial headers and razor-sharp, hyper-legible modern grotesk sans body copy.

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (Bodoni Moda):** Captures the cinematic luxury of title cards and couture magazines. Used with deliberate negative letter-spacing in oversized scales, and rendered in italic cuts for intimate romantic excerpts, quotes, and film subtitles.
- **Body & Functional Narrative (Manrope):** Delivers clean readability. Renders story synopsis, couple profiles, technical camera gear breakdowns, and booking itineraries.
- **Labels & Micro-Copy:** Styled exclusively in tracked uppercase (`0.15em` to `0.2em`) to echo film production metadata, slate boards, timestamps, and festival classifications.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an ultra-wide 12-column grid system tuned for 16:9 cinematic aspect ratios and immersive visual viewports.

- **Desktop (1200px+):** Fluid 12-column grid with generous 64px (`4rem`) outer margins and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters. Generous vertical rhythms allow still imagery and trailers room to breathe.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with 32px margins and 20px gutters. Film dual-panes stack cleanly while maintaining cinematic poster proportions.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) side margins and 16px (`1rem`) gutters. Gallery grids transition from multi-column masonry to full-bleed vertical reels.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface layering and low-contrast ambient borders rather than stark drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (The Void):** `#000000` canvas used for full-screen video players, lightboxes, and hero takeovers.
- **Level 1 (Ground Surface):** `#111111` backdrop applied across editorial body sections.
- **Level 2 (Film Cards & Overlays):** `#1a1a1a` with 1px hairline borders (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`) and diffuse ambient shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`).
- **Level 3 (Modal Screening & Menus):** Frosted glassmorphic panels using `rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and champagne tinted borders (`rgba(250, 209, 137, 0.15)`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a refined, subtle roundedness (Level 1: Soft). 

- Base UI interactive controls and film stills utilize an understated `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving the crisp geometry of physical cinema film slates and mounted photography mats.
- Medium components such as narrative callout boxes and booking panels use `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- Video screening frames and hero showcase images strictly preserve straight, architectural framing to mirror cinematic widescreen projection boundaries. Full pills are reserved solely for live status pills and audio playback scrub handles.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (The Premier Call):** Solid `#D60000` background, pure white text, uppercase `label-md` tracking. On hover: shifts to `#FF3333` with subtle glowing halo shadow (`0 0 20px rgba(214, 0, 0, 0.45)`).
- **Secondary (Editorial Outline):** Transparent background with 1px border of `#FAD189` and text in `#FAD189`. On hover: fills with `rgba(250, 209, 137, 0.1)`.
- **Cinematic Play Button:** Circular frosted icon button with center crimson play glyph; expands smoothly with audio-wave micro-animations on hover.

### Film & Portfolio Cards
- Constructed with `#1A1A1A` base, wrapped in hairline outlines (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`).
- Features dynamic 16:9 media preview containers that gently scale (`scale: 1.03`) on hover with smooth 500ms cubic-bezier easing.
- Bottom overlay holds Bodoni Moda film titles paired with metadata tags (Location, Year, Soundtrack) set in tracked Manrope uppercase labels.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#161616` with bottom-border-only or delicate all-around border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`).
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#FAD189` accompanied by a warm golden glow. Placeholder text in muted ash gray (`#808080`).

### Chips & Festival Laurels
- Compact badges in `#262626` background, featuring amber borders (`#CE8508`), enclosing film awards, destination tags, or exclusive premiere years.

### Media Player & Interactive Timeline
- Custom dark-mode video scrub bar: `#333333` track with `#D60000` buffered progress and `#FAD189` playhead thumb.
- Audio toggle buttons reveal live equalizer bars dancing to film background scores.
